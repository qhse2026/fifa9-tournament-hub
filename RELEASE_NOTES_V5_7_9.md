# FIFA Universe V5.7.9 — Grand Final Best of 3

## Official Final format
- M1: 4★
- M2: 4.5★
- M3: 5★ if required
- First player to 2 wins becomes FIFA 10 Champion.
- At 2–0, Match 3 is automatically marked `notRequired`.
- At 1–1, Match 3 remains playable and decides the championship.

## Preserved rules
- Third-place remains one match at 4.5★.
- Play-In, QF and SF results/draws are preserved.
- Unified Official Match Feed treats every completed Final-series game as a separate official FIFA 10 match.
- Team Passport records M1/M2/M3 team usage separately and keeps no-repeat team control.
- No new Supabase schema migration is required.
