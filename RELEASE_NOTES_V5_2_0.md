# FIFA Universe V5.2.0 — Spatial Universe Expansion

**Data becomes space. History becomes a place.**

V5.2 pauses the voice track and expands the FIFA Universe into a spatial visual operating layer. The release adds six data-driven environments: Universe Core, Spatial Trophy Museum, Player Galaxy, AI Comparison Chamber, Dynasty Corridor and Record Vault, plus Cinematic Tour.

The implementation deliberately leaves tournament result entry and core data engines untouched. Spatial scenes use official app APIs rather than parsing arbitrary rendered text.
