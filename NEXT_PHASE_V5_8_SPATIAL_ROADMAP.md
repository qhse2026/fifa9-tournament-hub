# FIFA Universe V5.8 — Final Night Broadcast & Living Bracket Roadmap

Checkpoint: V5.7 delivers Broadcast Command Deck, Spatial Bracket Chamber, Tournament Story Engine, observed-change story memory, Player Signature Core and Auto Broadcast.

## Recommended next development
1. Living Knockout Bracket Engine
   - Introduce an explicit official knockout data model for Play-In / QF / SF / 3rd Place / Final.
   - Never infer winners from unrelated data.
   - Make each official knockout tie a navigable spatial portal.
2. Broadcast Graphics Package 2.0
   - Dynamic lower-thirds, matchup cards, standings bugs and full-screen result boards.
   - TV mode and share-card export layouts.
3. Final Night Control Protocol
   - Once official finalists are known, transform the entire Spatial Universe into Final Night identity.
   - Tunnel, walkout, rivalry history, final stadium, podium and dynasty seal sequence.
4. Tournament Story Timeline
   - Preserve observed story events as a chronological FIFA 10 campaign journal.
   - Allow filtering by player, cutline, rivalry, record and result.
5. Persistent Player Signature 2.0
   - Apply the same deterministic visual identity tokens directly across Museum, Galaxy, Walkout, Rivalry Arena and Broadcast graphics.
6. Spatial Broadcast Director 4.0
   - Score story importance and compose an adaptive TV rundown from official data.
7. Optional WebGPU laboratory
   - Isolated particle / stadium light lab; never required by normal operation.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Result entry remains independent.
- No MutationObserver feedback loops.
- Official knockout winners/finalists must come from an explicit source-of-truth.
- Visual reconstruction must always be labelled when it is not an official event log.
