v44.9.9 FEATURE NOTES

GLOBAL LEADERBOARD
- Supabase RPC-backed Top 50 per circuit.
- Authenticated profile-name verification.
- Anonymous device-key entries without exposing raw identifiers.
- One best record per player/device and circuit.
- Faster laps update the record; slower attempts preserve the existing best.
- Local fallback when cloud or SQL is unavailable.

OFFICIAL TIME ATTACK
- Player-only session; no AI or collision traffic.
- Equal fixed vehicle specification, no mastery/R&D advantage.
- Dry weather, Soft tyre, four total laps: one out lap plus three flying laps.
- ERS, tyre wear and damage reset before every flying lap.
- Track limits and reset invalidate the current lap only.
- Automatic submission of the fastest valid lap.
